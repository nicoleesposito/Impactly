Your daily NGO impact measuring tool, all in one place

Impactly is your daily NGO impact measuring tool — track attendance, manage beneficiaries and report to funders, all in one place. Impactly is NGO impact management software built to help non-profits spend less time on admin and more time on the people they serve, built in direct partnership with a South African NGO, for NGOs who don’t have time to fight their tools.

Impactly is for NGO field staff, programme managers and administrators at small to mid-sized organisations. No dedicated IT team, no steep learning curve. If you’re currently running your programme out of a notebook, a shared spreadsheet and a WhatsApp group, Impactly replaces all of it with one connected system.

The platform brings attendance tracking, beneficiary management and funder reporting into a single toolkit. Mark who showed up in one tap, keep one record per beneficiary instead of five spreadsheets, and let funder reports build themselves from data you’ve already captured.

Most non-profits still rely on paper registers, spreadsheets and scattered WhatsApp messages to keep their programmes running. These outdated tools make it difficult to report accurately to funders, and they put years of beneficiary records at risk every time a staff member leaves. Impactly was built to solve exactly these challenges for non-government organisations across South Africa.
